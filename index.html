Sinkron Publisher

A personal publishing tool for the Sinkron TikTok channel.

Sinkron Publisher is a small desktop tool used by a single creator to upload their own short videos (mystery and history stories, produced with AI tools) to their own TikTok account.

The creator signs in with TikTok, chooses the video, caption and privacy settings, and the tool publishes the video using the official TikTok Content Posting API. The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's data.
